# HouseholdCracker's Vanilla+ Pack (Fabric 1.21.1)

**HouseholdCracker's Vanilla+ Pack** is a performance-oriented, vanilla-preserving modpack for Minecraft 1.21.1 built on the Fabric modloader. It keeps all core vanilla mechanics, progression, and world generation completely intact while dramatically improving frame rates, chunk loading speeds, visual atmosphere, and everyday quality of life.

***

### How This Pack Changes Your Experience

*   **Playable on Any Hardware:** By overhauling Minecraft's rendering engine and memory management, low-end PCs and laptops achieve high, stable frame rates without stuttering, while high-end setups gain the performance headroom needed for shaders and extreme view distances.
*   **Massive Horizons & Dynamic Atmosphere:** Instead of hitting a harsh fog wall a few chunks away, Level-of-Detail (LOD) terrain rendering lets you see distant mountains and landmarks across the world. Combined with dynamic torch lighting, connected textures, and shader support, exploration and building feel modern while staying true to Minecraft's art style.
*   **Cleaner Information Without Clutter:** Subtle HUD additions let you inspect blocks, entities, and exact food saturation values directly in-game without needing to alt-tab to a wiki or guess mechanics.
*   **100% Vanilla Gameplay Parity:** No new items, blocks, mobs, or altered recipes are added. All redstone contraptions, farms, and builds behave identically to standard vanilla Minecraft.

***

### Key Features & Included Mods

#### Performance & Optimization (All Editions)

*   **Sodium & Entity Culling:** Replaces the default rendering pipeline for major FPS gains and skips rendering mobs, chests, and signs hidden behind walls.
*   **Lithium, FerriteCore & ModernFix:** Optimizes game physics, mob AI, and memory (RAM) allocation while speeding up game launch times.

#### Visual Enhancements (Heavy Edition)

*   **Distant Horizons:** Renders simplified Level-of-Detail terrain far beyond the server's normal view distance with minimal performance cost, including server-side LOD synchronization.
*   **Iris Shaders:** Full support for modern shader packs alongside Sodium.
*   **Continuity:** Adds seamless connected textures for glass, sandstone, and other blocks.
*   **RyoamicLights:** Enables real-time dynamic lighting when holding torches, lanterns, or glowing items in your hand or dropping them on the ground.

#### Quality of Life (Heavy Edition)

*   **Jade:** Displays a clean, unobtrusive tooltip showing what block or entity you are looking at (e.g., crop growth percentage, container contents, or mob health).
*   **AppleSkin:** Visualizes saturation and exhaustion levels directly on your hunger bar so you know exactly how much healing food will provide.
*   **Better Third Person:** Unlocks 360-degree independent camera rotation in third-person view without changing movement controls.

***

### Which File Should You Download?

This project includes multiple variants under the **Files** tab so every player can match their hardware:

1.  **Heavy (Main File):** The complete experience containing all performance optimizations, quality-of-life utilities, and visual enhancements.
2.  **Light (Additional File):** A stripped-down, ultra-lightweight edition containing only core performance mods for maximum FPS on older or low-end computers.
3.  **Server Pack (Additional File):** Pre-configured server files featuring **C2ME**, **Noisium**, **Krypton**, **ServerCore**, and **Clumps** for multithreaded chunk generation, optimized network packets, and smooth multiplayer performance.
